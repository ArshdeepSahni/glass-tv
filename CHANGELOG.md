# Changelog

Each release, newest first. The lines under a version are what the TV and the phone show as what's new, so they're
short and plain. New lines go under `## Unreleased`: a title line, then `- ` lines. `tools/release.sh` turns that into
the version's section with the day it's released, so no date is typed here by hand.

## Unreleased

## 1.0.1

Glass on more TVs. 11 November 2026.

- Starts on Glass even on TVs that bring back their own home at every start, like Xiaomi's
- Brightness on every TV: where the TV keeps its backlight to itself, Glass dims the picture
- Wi-Fi in Control Center lists the networks, turning on Location when Android needs it
- Bluetooth says when it's turning on, and when the TV's Bluetooth has stopped
- The assistant button stays Glass's on TVs with 1 GB of memory, and the TV no longer asks which assistant
- The mic button opens Glass's assistant, listening, even on TVs where only Google hears the remote, like Xiaomi's
- Home on the phone remote always opens Glass
- Settings ▸ Speed & memory: free up memory, quicker animations, which apps run in the background, and the TV's own extras on or off

## 1.0.0

The first release. 9 November 2026.

- A calm home screen made of glass: your apps in a dock, widgets, and Control Center over any app
- Glass Assistant on the remote's assistant button: TV commands work offline, a free AI service answers the rest
- Your phone is the remote: scan the code on the TV, nothing to install
- Glass's own keyboard, six backgrounds and six screen savers, light by day and dark at night
- Updates from the TV: Settings › About this TV › Check for updates
- One command on your computer sets it all up: ./glass
