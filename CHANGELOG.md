# Changelog

One section per release. The heading is the version as in `launcher/app/build.gradle` (`versionName`, its first
word), and the `- ` lines under it are what the TV and the phone show as what's new, so they're short and in plain
words. `tools/release.sh` won't tag a version that has no section here.

## 5.1.2 — Tidier updates

- The downloaded update is cleared away once Glass has restarted on it

## 5.1.1 — The first update

- This version came to the TV through Glass's own update service
- Nothing else changed

## 5.1 — Updates

- Check for updates in Settings › About this TV, or on your phone's TV tab
- Updates download, check themselves and install without asking on the screen
- Glass restarts on the new version and says so

## 5.0 — Widgets

- Widgets you can move and resize: clock, weather, now playing, calendar and photos
- Weather for the town you choose, in Settings › Backgrounds & savers › Weather location
- Now playing from any app, with its artwork, on the desktop and on your phone
- Photos and videos sent from a phone open full screen, with a slideshow
- Light and Dark each keep their own background and screen saver
- Every text size fits; long names scroll when you land on them
- Phone remote: a dark look, your own shortcuts, artwork and media buttons

## Earlier

The Glass desktop and dock, Control Center, Settings, Files, the assistant and its button, the phone remote,
Glass's keyboard, the screen savers and the wake animation, on top of the open-source tvhome launcher.
